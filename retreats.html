Retreats & Events

What is Sesshin?

Sesshin means "to touch the mind" — to return to the original condition of the body-mind. Zen retreats vary from a half-day zazenkai to several days, consisting of zazen, samu (work practice), and meals. Longer sesshin may include dharma talks (teisho) and ordination ceremony.

Register at least one week in advance. Email noztinfo@gmail.com. Though beginners may attend, prior orientation is recommended.
