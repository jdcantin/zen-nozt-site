How to Start

Getting Started

New to Zen?

The New Orleans Zen Temple offers orientation lessons for beginning Zen practice. These cover posture, breathing, and the basic attitude of zazen, as well as dojo etiquette and ceremony. Proper instruction before participating in zazen is important.

How to Register

Email noztinfo@gmail.com with your name and contact information to register for an upcoming orientation. Please give a few days' notice, especially for groups of more than four.

Contribution for orientation and first zazen: $25. Please bring this on arrival.

What to Wear / Bring

  • Loose, dark clothing — no shorts or sleeveless tops
  • No fragrances or jewelry
  • Clean hands and feet before entering the dojo
  • An open and patient mind

What to Expect

The orientation covers posture, breathing, and attitude of mind, followed immediately by a practice period with the sangha. Total time approximately 90 minutes.

Zazen is not easy, especially at the beginning. But through patient daily repetition, anyone can practice Zen.
