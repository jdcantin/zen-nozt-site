Welcome

The New Orleans Zen Temple has provided instruction in true Zen practice for New Orleans and the Gulf South since 1983. We offer zazen sittings, retreats, and beginner orientation in the tradition of Master Taisen Deshimaru.

We are currently open for in-person zazen at 8338 Oak Street. Some sittings are also available on Zoom. Email noztinfo@gmail.com for information.
